# Interior imagery

Six images were created with the built-in imagegen tool for the requested interiors expansion. These are design concepts, not photographs of completed Wills Group projects. The public section identifies their purpose.

Delivery files are under public/media/wills/interiors/. Each scene has a 1440 x 810 WebP and a 640 x 360 responsive WebP. Original generated PNG files remain in the Codex generated_images directory. Existing supplied company photographs are retained.

Prompt set: one architectural interior photograph per scene, cohesive understated luxury in warm ivory, walnut, charcoal and subtle brass; natural daylight, realistic materials, buildable proportions, landscape framing, straight verticals; no people, text, logos, watermarks or collages.

- Living room: cream sectional seating, walnut wall panelling, stone media wall, slim black metal accents and warm concealed lighting.
- Bedroom: upholstered ivory bed, walnut headboard feature wall, fitted bedside cabinets and warm indirect ceiling lighting.
- Kitchen: walnut and warm ivory cabinets, stone island, brushed brass hardware and integrated storage.
- Dining room: walnut dining table, upholstered cream chairs, black metal framed glass partition and sculptural pendant lighting.
- Foyer: timber console, warm textured walls, large mirror and black metal stair balustrade.
- Wardrobe: walnut cabinetry, organized open shelving, warm integrated lighting and cream upholstered bench.
